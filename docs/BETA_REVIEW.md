# TiltCheck Beta Review Script

Use this for Matt and the first five structured reviewers.

**Review URL:** https://betting-insights-danielleemarlin.replit.app/demo  
**Target time:** 7–10 minutes  
**Account required:** No  
**Goal:** Determine whether the product thesis is understandable and compelling before asking the reviewer to use it with real bets.

## Before they open the link

Do not explain every feature. Give only this framing:

> TiltCheck is meant to help bettors judge the quality of their decisions, not just whether a bet won. Open the demo and tell me what you think it is, what feels useful, and what feels unnecessary.

Then let them drive.

## Review path

### 1. Dashboard

Ask:

- What do you think this product is for?
- What gets your attention first?
- Is anything immediately confusing or too busy?
- What would you click next without being told?

Observe rather than coaching them.

### 2. Stats

Ask:

- Do confidence vs. results and ROI feel actionable or just interesting?
- Does this change how you think about “being good” at betting?
- Which number or pattern would make you come back?

### 3. Lessons / leak detection

Ask:

- Does separating a bad decision from a losing result make sense?
- Do the repeated-mistake patterns feel credible?
- Would you actually tag your mistakes after losing?

### 4. Weekly Recap

Ask:

- Would you read this once a week if it used your own history?
- Does the tone feel useful, annoying, judgmental, or generic?
- Is this a reason to return to TiltCheck?

### 5. Crew / Workspace

Ask:

- Does seeing friends’ decision quality add accountability or noise?
- Would you invite anyone you actually bet with?
- Does this feel like a private crew tool or a social feed?

## Closing questions

Ask these exactly enough that the answer can be compared across reviewers:

1. In one sentence, what is TiltCheck?
2. What is the most valuable part?
3. What would you remove or hide?
4. What do you not trust yet?
5. Would you log your real bets in this for seven days?
6. If yes, what would make you stop using it?
7. Would you invite a betting friend?
8. Would you ever pay for this? If yes, what would have to be true first?

## What counts as signal

### Strong signal

- They ask to use it with their own bets.
- They return to a screen without prompting.
- They describe a specific habit the product could expose.
- They invite a betting friend.
- They use it for multiple betting sessions or days.
- They volunteer willingness to pay or ask what it costs.

### Weak signal

- “Cool idea.”
- Compliments on colors or polish without intended use.
- Feature requests before they have used the core loop.
- Saying they would use it “someday.”

## Build rule after five reviews

Do not average every opinion into a larger product. Look for repeated behavior.

Only promote a request into the roadmap when at least one of these is true:

- multiple testers hit the same blocker,
- the blocker prevents the core log → settle → grade → review loop,
- or a feature/screen repeatedly creates a clear return reason.
